CUSTOMER INFORMATION

Our Service Terms

These terms apply to appointments and services booked with Clip Your Crown Barbershop.

01

Appointments

Customers are responsible for providing accurate contact and appointment information when making an online booking. A booking is considered confirmed once the website displays a successful booking confirmation.

02

Arrival & Late Appointments

Customers are encouraged to arrive 5–10 minutes before their scheduled appointment. Late arrival may reduce the available service time or require the appointment to be rescheduled.

03

Cancellations

If you are unable to attend your appointment, please contact Clip Your Crown as early as possible so the appointment time can be made available to another customer.

04

Prices & Services

Prices displayed on the website apply to the listed services. Clip Your Crown may update services, durations and prices when necessary. Any applicable price will be shown before the customer confirms a booking.

05

Customer Information

Information submitted during booking is used for appointment administration and customer communication. Customers should ensure that their name, email address and telephone number are entered correctly.

06

Calendar Events

Calendar options are provided for convenience. Customers remain responsible for attending their appointment at the date and time confirmed during the booking process.

07

Service Expectations

Customers should communicate their preferred hairstyle or grooming requirements to their barber before the service begins. Results may vary depending on hair type, condition and requested style.

08

Contact

Questions regarding appointments or these terms can be directed to Clip Your Crown at bookings@clipyourcrown.co.za or 012 555 0147.
